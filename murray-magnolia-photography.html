Our Story

Unscripted life. Time art.

Mariah, 30, wife and mother of three from Columbus, Indiana, rediscovered photography in high school, restarted with small sessions in 2020, moved to Batesville in 2022, and officially launched Murray Magnolia Co. in 2026 after photographing her own children in winter 2024. Her promise is simple: “Real moments. Raw emotions. Lasting memories” — capturing the big moments and the beautiful in-between.
